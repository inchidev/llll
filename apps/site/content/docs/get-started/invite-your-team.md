New people join a Server through an invite link. Each person creates their own Account on your Server; Accounts on other Servers do not carry over.

## Create an invite link

You need the **Create invites** permission. The Owner has it.

1. In your Server, open **Server settings → Invites**.
2. Select **Create invite**.
3. Select **Copy** next to the link and send it to the people you want to invite.

![The Invites settings page with the Create invite button and the Invite link panel showing a link and a Copy button](screenshot:get-started-invite-link "Create and copy an invite link")

The Invites list shows each link's code, how many times it was used and when it expires. A link created here has no use limit and never expires, so share it only with people you want in the Server.

## What the invited person does

1. They open the invite link. It shows **Join server** and your Server's name.
2. If they are not signed in, they select **Sign in to join**, then **Create account** to make an Account on your Server.
3. After signing in, they select **Join**.

They can also paste the invite link into the Shell's **Connect** tab to save the Server on their device. See [Connect to a Server](connect-a-server.md).

> [!NOTE]
> A Server starts with registration set to **Invite only**. The Owner can change it in **Server settings → Authentication → Registration** to **Open signup**.

## Give people the right Role

People who join through an invite get the **Member** Role. By default it lets them send messages, attach files and start Live.

To give someone more access:

1. Open **Server settings → Members** and select the person.
2. Choose a **Server role**.

To shape what each Role can do, or give access only inside one Project, use **Server settings → Roles & Policies**. See [Accounts, Members and Roles](../concepts/accounts-members-roles.md).

To let people talk to Agents, their Role needs **Use agents**. See [Create your first Agent](first-agent.md).

## Remove someone

Open **Server settings → Members**, select the person and choose **Remove member**. You need the permission to remove members, and the Owner cannot be removed.
