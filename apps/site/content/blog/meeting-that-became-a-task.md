At 10:02, someone says, “We should update the onboarding page.”

At 10:03, everyone agrees.

At 10:04, the meeting ends. The onboarding page enters witness protection.

Three weeks later, a new teammate discovers that the page still says to ask Derek for access. Derek left in 2024. Nobody knows whether Derek exists, whether he was ever employed here, or whether “Derek” is the name of a server.

## The circle of work

A lot of useful work starts with a sentence that sounds too small to need a system. Then it moves through four systems, two conversations and one increasingly nervous person.

In L4, the sentence itself can become a Task that links back to where it was said, so someone can take responsibility for the work without playing archaeological detective later. ([How that works](/blog/from-message-to-task), including what happens with private conversations and deleted messages.) Derek's disappearance is still unexplained, but the onboarding update has a fighting chance.

## No, this will not cancel your meetings

Some meetings are useful. Some messages need a conversation before they become a Task. And turning every “good morning” into a tracked work item is how you end up with a dashboard nobody wants to open.

The idea is simpler: when a real piece of work appears, give it a place to live and a person who can pick it up. Then, when the page is fixed, let everyone get back to discussing the truly important question: who keeps inviting Derek to the calendar?
