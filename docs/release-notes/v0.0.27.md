# L4 0.0.27

A dropped or overloaded model call is asked again instead of ending the Agent's turn. No migration.

## Agents

- **Retries on the box.** When a model call fails with a transient error (an overloaded provider, a 5xx or 429, a broken or ended stream, a timeout), the Agent's box asks again up to three times with backoff, and the turn goes on. Before, one failed call ended a turn however much work it had done. A provider account without credit, a quota or a refusal is not retried.
- **Clearer failure.** A generic model failure now reads "The model service is temporarily unavailable, or its answer was interrupted".

## Updating

- No migration, no binding change. Hosted Servers update in place. L4 Compute needs the 0.0.27 box templates.
