# L4 0.0.34

Portal: share one window of your Mac into a conversation. L4 is now paid for by Usage. No database migration; one new Durable Object.

## Portal (experimental)

- **Share a window.** The window button in a channel or Direct Message opens a Portal: a guest link that works in any browser without an account, and a link that connects the PartyPortal app on your Mac. Only that window is sent. What the guest may see or do is decided on your Mac: control stays off until you allow it there, and a key on your Mac takes it back.
- **Direct, not through the Server.** The picture goes peer to peer between the Mac and the guest whenever the network allows it. The Server serves the guest page at `/portal/<id>`, checks two keys given out once (the guest's in the link's fragment, the host's in the `partyportal://` link), and passes the signals that connect the two.
- **TURN when there is no direct path.** The Server gives the host short-lived relay credentials from a Cloudflare Realtime TURN key: `REALTIME_TURN_KEY_ID` and `REALTIME_TURN_KEY_API_TOKEN`, or one the Server makes once in its own Cloudflare account when it keeps Cloudflare access. Relayed traffic is billed to that account.
- **One guest at a time.** A newer guest replaces the older one. When the host's Mac disconnects, the guest waits and continues when it comes back. A Portal ends when its opener or a channel manager ends it, when the opener can no longer start Live in the conversation, or after twelve hours.
- **In the Mac app** the page asks and the app decides: it checks that the Portal belongs to the Server on screen and asks you in its own dialog before PartyPortal opens.
- Opening one needs **Start live sessions**, the same as a call. See [ADR 0014](../adr/0014-portal.md) for the design and the stages still to come.

## Usage

- [ADR 0009](../adr/0009-compute-as-the-business.md) is amended: the workspace stays free, and L4 charges only for what burns resources (boxes, models, files and video above a free base) at cost plus a stated margin, through a subscription that includes use or a prepaid balance. Prices are on llll.gg/pricing.

## Updating

- No migration. The update adds the `PORTAL_DO` Durable Object binding (migration tag `v8-portal`); the installer and hosted updates add it. A Server that has not updated answers "This Server was installed before Portal" when someone tries to open one.
